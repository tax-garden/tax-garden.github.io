# GSTR-3B Filing Guide for Indian Businesses: Deadlines, Penalties, and ITC Rules (2026)

If you run a business registered under GST, filing GSTR-3B is your most time-sensitive monthly obligation.

Most businesses treat it as a formality. Smart businesses use it to avoid penalties, claim proper Input Tax Credit (ITC), and stay compliant.

This guide explains GSTR-3B filing, due dates, penalties, and ITC rules for FY 2026–27.

---

## What is GSTR-3B?

GSTR-3B is a summary GST return used to:

- Report sales
- Report purchases
- Claim ITC
- Pay GST liability

It is filed monthly or quarterly depending on your GST scheme.

---

## Who Must File GSTR-3B?

Mandatory for:

- Normal taxpayers
- Casual taxable persons

Even if there are no sales or purchases:

You must file a NIL return.

---

## GSTR-3B Due Dates for FY 2026–27

### Monthly Filers

- Due Date: 20th of next month

Example:

April 2026 return → Due by 20 May 2026

---

## QRMP Quarterly Filers

For turnover up to Rs 5 crore.

### PMT-06 Payment

- Due by 25th of next month

### GSTR-3B Filing

#### Category A States

- Due by 22nd after quarter

#### Category B States

- Due by 24th after quarter

---

## Late Fees and Interest

### Late Fees

#### Standard Return

- Rs 50 per day

#### NIL Return

- Rs 20 per day

---

## Interest

- 18% per annum
- Applied on cash tax liability

---

## ITC Rules You Must Know

### 1. Invoice Must Appear in GSTR-2B

If supplier does not file GSTR-1:

You cannot claim ITC.

---

### 2. Claim ITC Within Deadline

ITC must be claimed before:

- 30 November of following year

OR

- Before filing GSTR-9

Whichever is earlier.

---

### 3. Business Use Only

No ITC on:

- Personal expenses
- Personal vehicles
- Certain employee expenses

---

# Step-by-Step Filing Process

1. Login to GST portal
2. Open Returns Dashboard
3. Select tax period
4. Fill GSTR-3B tables
5. Verify liability
6. Pay tax
7. File using DSC or OTP

---

# Why Timely Filing Matters

Late filing can cause:

- Penalties
- Interest
- GST notices
- ITC mismatch
- GSTIN suspension
